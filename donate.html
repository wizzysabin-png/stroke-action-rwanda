Your Support Saves Lives

Stroke is a leading cause of mortality and long-term disability. It places a significant medical, social, and economic burden on individuals, families, and health systems. Your donation supports evidence-based stroke prevention, early recognition initiatives, rehabilitation services, and community-based recovery programs across Rwanda. By investing in stroke action, you contribute to reducing preventable deaths, minimizing disability, and strengthening organized stroke care systems.

Why Your Contribution Matters

Stroke is largely preventable, treatable when managed rapidly, and recoverable with appropriate rehabilitation. Sustainable progress depends on strong community awareness, trained health professionals, and structured post-stroke support services. Every contribution directly strengthens our capacity to reach underserved and high-risk populations with life-saving education and support aligned with global stroke care standards.

  • Prevention:Supporting population-level risk reduction strategies, including blood pressure awareness, lifestyle education, and recognition of early neurological warning signs.
  • Education: Building healthcare capacity through training in stroke identification, referral pathways, and evidence-informed acute stroke response.
  • Recovery: Expanding access to multidisciplinary rehabilitation resources that improve functional independence and long-term outcomes.
  • Support: Expanding access to multidisciplinary rehabilitation resources that improve functional independence and long-term outcomes.
